Collaborative · 2026

xLaDe — Lean 4 ecosystem framework

A reproducibility framework that tracks toolchain metadata, dependencies, and execution context for long-term preservation of Lean 4 formalization projects. I contributed to the metadata, policy-enforcement, and experiment-management layers.

Research project · 2026

Second-order ODEs via machine learning

Analytical and computational methods for solving second-order ordinary differential equations, benchmarking classical numerical schemes against machine-learning-based approximators. Presented at ICAMDS 2026.
